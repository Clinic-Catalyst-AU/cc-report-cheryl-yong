How it all connects

Every booking tells the ads what worked

Google or Meta ad→Landing page→GoHighLevel→Answered and followed up→Booked in Dentally or Timely→Booking flows back→Meta and Google learn

Dentally and Timely stay exactly as they are. Zapier brings each new booking back into GoHighLevel, matched to the enquiry, and GoHighLevel tells Meta and Google which ads produced real appointments. So the campaigns optimise for patients who book, not for clicks, and the reconciliation your team does by hand today happens on its own.

Only what marketing needs travels through it: contact details and whether they booked, attended and went ahead. Clinical notes stay in Dentally.
